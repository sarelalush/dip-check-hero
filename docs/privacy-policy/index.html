Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AquaSense collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile app for scanning pool water test strips and managing pool test results.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as email address and display name.
  • Pool details that you choose to save in the app.
  • Water test results, history, and related app data.
  • Images you upload or capture for test strip analysis.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the app.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide test strip scanning and water balance guidance.
  • Save your pools and test history.
  • Improve app reliability, security, and user experience.
  • Provide support and respond to user requests.

Camera and photos

The app may request access to your camera or photo library so you can capture or upload pool test strip images. These images are used to analyze test strip colors and provide results.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the app, process app functionality, provide cloud storage, authentication, analytics, support, or comply with legal obligations.

Data retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the app, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at: Delete account and data.

Children

AquaSense is not intended for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data against unauthorized access.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request account/data deletion, contact us at support@dipcheck.app.
