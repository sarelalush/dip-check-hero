Delete account and data

This page explains how users can request deletion of their StickCheck Water account and the data associated with it. StickCheck Water is published by the developer account shown on Google Play.

Email deletion request

Steps to request account deletion

  1. Email us at support@dipcheck.app with the subject “Account deletion request”.
  2. Send the request from the email address connected to your StickCheck Water account, or include the account email/user ID in the message.
  3. We may contact you to verify account ownership before deleting the account.
  4. After verification, we will delete your account and associated data within 30 days, unless retention is legally required.

What to include

Send the request from the email address connected to your AquaSense account, or include the account email/user ID in your message.

Processing time

We will process verified deletion requests within 30 days and may contact you if we need to confirm account ownership.

Data we delete

Deletion includes your app account, saved pools, water test history, uploaded scan images, and app data linked to your account.

Limited retention

Some records may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, billing, or tax obligations.

Delete from inside the app

If you still have access to the app, open AquaSense and go to Settings > Delete account.

You can also contact us directly at support@dipcheck.app.

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.
